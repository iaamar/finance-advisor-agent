1

Before a client conversation, an advisor has to check a stock's price by hand and then read long SEC filings. Annual reports typically run 100 to 300-plus pages; a single NVIDIA annual report (10-K) is roughly 340,000 characters. That is slow, every advisor does it a little differently, and the warning signs compliance cares most about, such as auditor problems, restatements or heavy insider selling, are easy to miss when skimming.
