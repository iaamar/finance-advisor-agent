Advisor Research Desk

3

Top band: the assistant reads the question, works out which company it is about and which workflow or workflows are needed, runs them, and returns one answer with sources. It keeps short-term memory of the conversation, so a follow-up like "any insider selling?" stays on the same company. Workflow 1, stock quote: match the company name to its official ticker, fetch six months of daily prices, and calculate the current price, six-month change and high/low with ordinary arithmetic. The output is the price plus a one-paragraph summary. The AI never produces these numbers. Workflow 2, SEC filings: find the company in the SEC's official list, then read four report types at the same time: the annual report (10-K, or 20-F for foreign companies), the quarterly report (10-Q), current-event reports (8-K or 6-K: earnings releases, deals, executive changes) and insider trades (Form 4). We keep management's discussion of results, top company-specific risks, outlook, buybacks, legal matters and insider buying and selling, and skip boilerplate. Red flags (control weaknesses, restatements, going-concern doubts, auditor changes) are checked by fixed rules, not AI. Key financials come from the structured financial data the SEC publishes. The output is one combined summary.
